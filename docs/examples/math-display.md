# Display math

With graphics on, each formula below is typeset as an image. Elsewhere, and
for any formula that cannot be typeset, the source is shown as text.

## Fractions

$$
\frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad + bc}{bd}
$$

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

$$
\cfrac{1}{1 + \cfrac{1}{1 + \cfrac{1}{x}}}
$$

## Sums, products and integrals with limits

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

$$
\prod_{p \text{ prime}} \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}} = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s}
$$

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2} \, dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1
$$

## Matrices

$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} =
\begin{pmatrix} ax + by \\ cx + dy \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\det \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 10 \end{vmatrix} = -3
$$

$$
I_3 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

## Aligned equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
(a + b)^2 &= (a + b)(a + b) \\
          &= a^2 + 2ab + b^2
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 & \text{if } x \ge 0 \\ -x & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
$$

## Symbols, accents and delimiters

$$
\nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}, \qquad
\nabla \times \vec{B} - \frac{1}{c^2} \frac{\partial \vec{E}}{\partial t} = \mu_0 \vec{J}
$$

$$
\left( \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_k b_k \right)^2 \le
\left( \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_k^2 \right) \left( \sum_{k=1}^{n} b_k^2 \right)
$$

$$
\hat{x}, \tilde{y}, \bar{z}, \overline{AB}, \underbrace{1 + \cdots + 1}_{n}
$$

$$
\mathbb{R}, \mathcal{L}, \mathfrak{g}, \mathbf{v}, \operatorname{tr}(A)
$$

$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$
